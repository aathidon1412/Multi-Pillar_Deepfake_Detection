7. Detailed comments: Please justify your recommendation and suggest improvements in technical content or presentation.
The five-pillar concept with a deterministic override rule for handling domain shift is genuinely novel, and the ViT ablation (65.6%→98.9%
accuracy) is a strong result. However the manuscript is incomplete — four literature-review entries are left as raw placeholders ("[author to fill
in: ...]"), and only the ViT and acoustic/Benford pillars have full experimental backing; the rPPG and geometry pillars, and critically the fused
end-to-end override mechanism, are never evaluated together on a real multimodal dataset.
8. Recommended changes: Please indicate any changes that should be made to the paper
Fill in all placeholder references before resubmission; provide an end-to-end evaluation of the fusion/override rule on multimodal data, or
explicitly scope the paper to the ViT pillar and present the rest as future work; add proper ROC/PR results for the rPPG and geometry pillars
instead of single threshold statements.

7. Detailed comments: Please justify your recommendation and suggest improvements in technical content or presentation.
The multi-pillar fusion concept with deterministic override for domain-shift robustness is a genuinely useful contribution. However, only Pillar 1
(ViT) is rigorously evaluated with ablation and baselines; the other four pillars report only isolated summary statistics without integrated end-toend evaluation of the fusion/override mechanism. The literature review contains several "[author to fill in]" placeholders that must be completed.
Table II mixes accuracy and AUC across rows/protocols inconsistently, which needs clarification.
8. Recommended changes: Please indicate any changes that should be made to the paper
Complete all placeholder citations in Section II; make Table II metrics consistent across models; provide at least a small-scale end-to-end
fusion evaluation or explicitly reframe the contribution as partially validated; proofread abstract/intro.
